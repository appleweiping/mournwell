# Changelog

## 1.1.0 - 2026-10-23

- Depth II, the Drowned Belfry: beating the first Tollmother opens a well
  down to a second layout with a second Tollmother.
- Two new enemies on Depth II: the ashmoth and the chimewraith.
- The toll bell (Space or the TOLL button): 15 resolve for 45 damage to every
  enemy in the chamber, with an 8 second cooldown.
- Removed the process docs and the exact npm version requirement.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-08-02

### Added

- Complete title, exploration, death, restart, Boss, and victory flow.
- Deterministic connected 5–8 room dungeons with treasure and farthest-leaf
  Boss chambers, persistent clear state, sealed doors, and a minimap.
- Four original enemies, including the three-phase Tollmother encounter.
- Eight stackable relics and drift-free stat derivation.
- Keyboard and simultaneous two-pointer mobile controls.
- Procedurally drawn art and synthesized Web Audio effects with no downloaded
  production assets.
- Read-only `window.__game` observability contract plus development-only QA
  fixtures.
- Unit tests, Playwright desktop/mobile journeys, CI and GitHub Pages
  deployment.
